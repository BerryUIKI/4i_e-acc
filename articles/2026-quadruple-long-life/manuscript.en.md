# Quadruple Long on Cognition, Long on Life for the Long Run

> Status: Draft · English translation (tentative title)

## Opening (hook + thesis)
In the markets some pile on leverage; others forget to lever up their own
"cognition." This essay argues that cognition is the only asset worth legging
into for the long run — go "4× long" on thinking, and stay "long" on life.

## Body
### 1. What "long on cognition" means
Treat cognition as an asset: it compounds, it does not rely on luck. Every
deliberate thought is adding to a position in your future self.

### 2. Why "four times"
Diversification brings mediocrity; focus brings excess return. "Four times" is
an stance toward focus and compounding — concentrate finite energy on the
highest-leverage cognition.

### 3. Long-termism: time is the long's best friend
Short-term noise, long-term signal. Run life as a long position; trade time for
certainty.

### 4. Risk and drawdown: cognition also pulls back
Cognition ages and draws down. The point is not to avoid mistakes, but to set
stops and keep iterating.

## Closing (action / reflection)
Run life as a long position: stop shorting yourself, and keep betting on
cognition.
