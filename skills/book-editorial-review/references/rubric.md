# Evaluation Rubric

Apply these 8 criteria to every chapter.

## 1. Book Quality (书籍感)

Does the chapter read like a real published book? Or does it feel like:
- A blog article (博客文章)
- WeChat public account article (微信公众号文章)
- Xiaohongshu post (小红书内容)
- AI-generated explanation (AI生成的解释)
- Lecture notes (课堂笔记)
- Investment tutorial (投资教程)

**Key indicators of "blog feel":**
- Paragraphs of 1-2 sentences each with excessive line breaks
- Lack of paragraph development — each idea is stated but not explored
- Abrupt transitions between ideas without connective tissue
- Cliched openings ("很多人觉得...", "其实...")

**Key indicators of "book feel":**
- Developed paragraphs (3-6 sentences) that build an argument
- Smooth transitions that create forward momentum
- Varied sentence rhythm — short sentences punctuate, long sentences explain
- The reader feels guided, not lectured

## 2. Depth (认知深度)

Does the chapter build cognition, or only explain concepts?

**Surface level (score < 5):**
- Defines what something is but doesn't explain why it matters
- States conclusions without showing reasoning
- Repeats common knowledge without adding new perspective

**Cognitive depth (score ≥ 7):**
- Changes how the reader thinks, not just what they know
- Reveals non-obvious connections between ideas
- Addresses counterarguments or common misconceptions
- Provides frameworks the reader can apply independently

The book should primarily help readers think differently rather than merely teach definitions.

## 3. Narrative Flow (叙事���辑)

Does the chapter have a natural progression, or is it merely:
```
Point A ↓
Point B ↓
Point C ↓
Summary
```

**Good narrative flow:**
- Each section builds on the previous one
- The reader understands why section B follows section A
- The chapter has a climax or turning point
- The ending resolves and connects back to the beginning

**Poor narrative flow:**
- Sections are interchangeable — reordering wouldn't change anything
- The chapter feels like a bullet list expanded into prose
- The reader could stop halfway through and miss nothing essential

## 4. Paragraph Style (段落风格)

Check whether paragraphs are too short.

**Problem:** One sentence per paragraph. One idea per line. Excessive white space. This is social media formatting, not book writing.

**Good practice:** Longer paragraphs (3-6 sentences) with smooth transitions. Paragraph breaks signal topic shifts, not sentence endings.

**Exception:** The glossary/tool-introduction chapter may use shorter paragraphs if each paragraph introduces a different instrument.

## 5. Repetition (重复度)

Identify repeated ideas across chapters. Mark duplicated content.

Common offenders in investment books:
- "Diversification is important" (分散投资很重要)
- "Invest for the long term" (长期投资)
- "Risk is not volatility" (风险不是波动)
- "Asset allocation matters" (资产配置很重要)
- "Discipline beats intelligence" (纪律比聪明重要)
- "Time is your greatest advantage" (时间是你最大的优势)

**Scoring:**
- Each occurrence after the canonical chapter is a demerit
- If the same idea appears in 5+ chapters, flag as a structural problem
- Distinguish between "intentional echo" (brief callback) and "unintentional duplication" (full restatement)

## 6. Timelessness (永恒性)

A good investing book should still be readable ten years from now.

**Tactical content to minimize (unless absolutely necessary):**
- Fixed allocation percentages (e.g. "always hold 60% stocks")
- Specific ETF tickers or product names
- Current market commentary or recent events as primary examples
- References to current platforms, apps, or interfaces

**Timeless content to maximize:**
- Principles and mental models
- Historical case studies (events that have already aged well)
- Behavioral insights that don't change with market conditions
- Universal patterns rather than current tactics

## 7. Emotional Tone (情绪基调)

Target tone: 《金钱心理学》(The Psychology of Money), 《漫步华尔街》(A Random Walk Down Wall Street), 《穷查理宝典》(Poor Charlie's Almanack)

**Acceptable:**
- Warm but analytical
- Authoritative but humble
- Conversational but precise
- Respectful of the reader's intelligence

**Unacceptable:**
- Motivational preaching (成功学/鸡汤)
- Internet influencer style (网红自媒体语气)
- Over-familiarity that feels unearned
- Excessive self-deprecation that undermines credibility

## 8. Extra Sections (茶话会/专栏)

Evaluate every recurring extra section (e.g. 茶话会, sidebars, Q&A).

**Value-adding:**
- Provides a genuine personal story not covered in the chapter
- Offers a different angle or counterpoint
- Contains a concrete, actionable takeaway
- Shows vulnerability or real experience

**Redundant:**
- Restates the chapter's main point in a different voice
- Uses the same opening formula every time
- Feels like filler content to hit a structural requirement
- Could be deleted without losing anything essential

## Scoring

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 8-10 | Publishable as-is. Strong in all dimensions. |
| 6-7 | Good foundation, needs specific improvements. |
| 4-5 | Has direction but lacks substance or polish. |
| 2-3 | Major structural problems. Needs rewriting. |
| 0-1 | Unsalvageable. Discard and start over. |

## Verdict Categories

- **Keep** — Minor proofreading only
- **Minor Revision** — Strengthen specific sections, add data/examples
- **Major Rewrite** — Restructure significantly, improve depth and style
- **Rewrite from Scratch** — Current version is not salvageable; start with a new approach
